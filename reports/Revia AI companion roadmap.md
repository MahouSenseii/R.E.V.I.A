# Give Revia a stage and a bigger brain

Revia's inner life is already more rigorous than that of any open-source AI VTuber surveyed. She has evidence-gated relationships, a 20-component emotion model with persistent mood, initiative that can choose silence, hybrid SQLite memory and a typed, audited capability policy, and most of it is **Verified live**. Nearly everything the outside world would see is missing: **no avatar renderer, no OBS, Twitch or YouTube connector, no backend besides llama.cpp and no MCP**. Her speech is synthesized a sentence at a time, so the first sentence arrives after 1.2–2.0 s, she understands only English, and her 4B Main model runs at an 8K context that her own roadmap says "performs personality instead of delivering substance". The gap to ChatGPT and Claude splits in two. In conversation, a 27–35B local model comes within about 56 Elo of the best frontier model. On agentic, knowledge-heavy work, the best model that fits her 20 GB of VRAM scores **34 against frontier's 58** on the independent Artificial Analysis index, so frontier-level capability needs a hybrid "local voice, cloud brain" route that costs about a cent per escalated turn. The highest-leverage order has four steps: fix foundations first (replace the brand-new running history summary, which is exposed to "context collapse", with a dated observation log; add a provider interface; upgrade the local model); build her a stage (streaming TTS, semantic turn-taking, Live2D through VTube Studio, and Twitch, YouTube and OBS behind a stream-safety layer); give her a brain on call and hands (a cloud advisor route, MCP skills, hosted coding agents, speaker identification); and only then add a VM stage for games and browsing, VRM, and cautious weight-level learning. Nearly every step plugs into a seam she already has, such as the presentation bus, avatar bridge, Presence `stream` source, `CapabilityPolicy`, `IReviaSkill`, conversation archive and relationship registry, which is what makes the plan feasible for one developer. The binding constraints are legal rather than technical: she must stay openly an AI and non-manipulative (California SB 243 and the providers' own rules), cloud outputs may not be distilled into her chat model, Twitch does not allow covers over ripped instrumentals, and voiceprints must stay local and be collected with consent. Overall she grades a **C** today: an A− for safety engineering and B-range marks for memory and personality, pulled down by D-range marks for streaming, games, coding and backend pluggability.

## Revia's inner life already outclasses most open companions

Revia is not a thin chatbot wrapper. By a line count taken for this report, the repository holds about 111,000 lines of C++20/Qt 6 product code and 43,000 lines of tests. One `ReviaSession` supervises separate loopback worker processes for three llama.cpp model tiers, embeddings, whisper.cpp, Qwen3-TTS and a visible browser, so a worker crash cannot take her down ([README](../README.md)). By the README's own vocabulary, her mind is live. The following are all **Verified live**: streaming chat with a 0.4–0.5 s time to first token; persistent personality, mood, likes and relationships; curiosity and self-directed reflection; SQLite-plus-embedding memory; continuous multi-monitor screen awareness; a cloned Qwen3-TTS voice; hands-free recognition; and visible-browser web lookups at about 11 s each. The following are **Tested** only: the `/improve` self-review loop, karaoke, desktop control, barge-in, the camera, SD-Turbo images, and the Discord voice connector (18/18 tests, but it has never joined a live call). The animated avatar, OBS and every streaming platform are **Not yet** ([README](../README.md)).

Several design choices put her ahead of the field. Her relationships move only on evidence: "A model never assigns relationship numbers … claiming to be trusted produces no trust", warmth is capped by familiarity, and reaching "you know them well, you like them, you trust them" takes about 400 exchanges ([ROADMAP](../docs/ROADMAP.md)). Grok's companions, by contrast, scored each interaction from roughly −10 to +15 on an affection meter that unlocked outfits, which one reviewer called "the single most addictive design choice in any companion app tested" ([PocketAnimus](https://pocketanimus.com/guides/grok-companions/)). Her initiative is capped at four unprompted utterances an hour, at least 15 minutes apart and never over full-screen apps or calls ([README](../README.md)), in line with Alexa+'s aim to "fade into the background" ([About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-generative-artificial-intelligence)). Every action is a typed `ActionRequest` that must pass `CapabilityPolicy` and human confirmation, with a durable audit intent written before the executor runs ([ARCHITECTURE](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)), and "model text never becomes a shell command" ([README](../README.md)). Public and private are already separated: Presence turns from Discord, a stream or a game get channel-scoped history only, never private memory, the desktop or the camera ([Presence README](../Tools/Presence/README.md)), which is exactly the split an AI needs when it is both a private assistant and a public VTuber.

The open-source VTuber projects have the surfaces she lacks. Project AIRI (about 49.7k stars, MIT) ships Live2D and VRM, Minecraft and Factorio agents, Discord and Telegram voice, and 25+ LLM APIs ([AIRI](https://github.com/moeru-ai/airi)); Open-LLM-VTuber adds Live2D expression mapping, a desktop-pet mode, proactive speaking and MCP tools ([Open-LLM-VTuber](https://github.com/Open-LLM-VTuber/Open-LLM-VTuber)); and handcrafted-persona-engine, a native Windows app with a built-in Live2D renderer, phoneme lip sync and Spout output to OBS, is the closest architectural match to Revia ([persona-engine](https://github.com/fagenorn/handcrafted-persona-engine)). None of them documents a relationship, provenance or permission model comparable to hers. ## A 4B model at 8K context caps every capability above it

Revia's roadmap records the symptom. Asked three separate technical questions, she "produced a characterful comment about the topic and never answered it ('A mutex ... is basically a digital doorman.' and nothing further)". Adding a rule to her profile prompt did not fix it ([ROADMAP](../docs/ROADMAP.md)). The cause is structural. Main, Fast and Expert all run at **8,192 tokens of context** ([settings.json](../Config/settings.json)), the system prompt alone takes about 5 KB of it (per the commit message of `8707ffd`), and small models know little. On AA-Omniscience, Qwen3.5 4B, the family her Main model belongs to, has an **80% hallucination rate** at 12.8% accuracy, and the 9B has 82% ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/qwen3-5-small-models)).

Independent numbers point two ways. The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2 is weighted toward agentic and knowledge-heavy work, and on it Claude Opus 5.5 (max) scores **58**. The best open-weight models reach 42–46, but they are 1T-class mixtures of experts that she could only use through the cloud. The best model that fits her hardware, **Qwen3.8 27B, scores 34**, against 14 for Gemma 4 12B and 11 for Qwen3.5 9B ([AA leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models); [AA Qwen3.8 27B](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b)). On human chat preference the gap is small. LMArena puts gemma-4-31b at 1453 against claude-opus-5.5-high at 1509 ([arena.ai](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/text)), a 56-Elo gap that works out to about a 42/58 head-to-head. **Local conversation can reach near-parity; local agentic and factual work cannot.**

### Upgrade the local model first, within the voice budget

Moving from the 4B to the 27–35B class is the cheapest single capability jump. Revia already runs GGUF models through `llama-server`, so the upgrade is mostly a model swap plus template and sampler settings. The candidates are Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (Apache-2.0; 3B active parameters, vision, a 262K native context and a vendor-reported 73.4 on SWE-bench Verified) ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B)), the dense Qwen3.8-27B (Apache-2.0, with vision and a vendor-reported 84.3 on OSWorld-Verified) ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B)), and Gemma 4 12B (Apache-2.0, with 256K context and audio input) ([Gemma 4 card](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core/model_card_4)).

The binding constraint is **VRAM shared with her voice**. Qwen3-TTS runs on both cards and needs about 4.6 GB free on each, and chat-model splitting across cards is disabled ([settings.json](../Config/settings.json)). A 27B dense model at Q4 is about 16.8 GB ([localmodel.run](https://localmodel.run/model/qwen3.6-27b)), so it would take both GPUs and push the voice out. The 35B-A3B needs about 17 GB at 3-bit and 23 GB at 4-bit ([Unsloth](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.6)). Because only 3B of its parameters are active per token, it can keep attention on the RTX 5070 and offload its expert layers to system RAM. The exact flags, and the speed this mixed pair of cards would reach, are unverified.

The practical layout puts the MoE as Main on the RTX 5070 with its experts partly offloaded to system RAM, leaves the RTX 2070 Super running TTS and speech recognition plus the current 4B as a side model for memory evaluation, summarization and routing, and keeps Gemma 4 12B, running entirely on the 5070, as the fallback.

The roadmap's still-open benchmarks for the KV cache and offloading serve as the acceptance test ([ROADMAP](../docs/ROADMAP.md)). Two settings come almost free. Multi-token-prediction speculative decoding, which llama.cpp merged in May 2026, gives 1.4–2.2x faster generation on Qwen3.6 ([PR #22673](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/22673)). Thinking should be turned off for persona turns, because a study of 24 LLMs across six role-play benchmarks found chain-of-thought "may reduce role-playing performance" ([Feng et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16940)).

### Replace the hard-wired llama.cpp client with a provider interface

`llmService` holds a concrete `llamaCppService` member, and `messageRouter` holds exactly three `llmService` instances ([llmService.h](../Public/LLM/llmService.h); [messageRouter.h](../Public/Core/messageRouter.h)). The fix is an `IChatProvider` interface with three parts: discovered capabilities (tools, strict schemas, grammar, vision, reasoning control, prompt caching, a tokenize endpoint), a canonical request built from typed message parts, and a streaming event type that generalizes today's `DeltaHandler`.

Two adapters cover almost everything. An OpenAI Chat Completions adapter reaches llama-server, Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM (through WSL2) and OpenRouter. An Anthropic Messages adapter is required for serious cloud Claude use. Anthropic's OpenAI-compatibility layer ignores `strict` on tools and `response_format`, does not support prompt caching, and does not return thinking text ([Anthropic docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/openai-sdk)). The same adapter also reaches llama.cpp locally, which now serves `/v1/messages` ([HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/anthropic-messages-api-in-llamacpp)).

Revia's existing task methods then become named routes (`chat.persona`, `memory.evaluate`, `code.review`, `vision.analyze`). Each route carries a provider, a model and a privacy tier (LocalOnly, CloudAllowed or CloudRedacted). The router enforces the tier, not the adapter, and a visible indicator appears whenever a turn leaves the machine. Do not bundle a Python gateway. In March 2026, malicious LiteLLM releases that stole SSH keys and cloud credentials sat on PyPI for about 40 minutes and were downloaded more than 119k times ([PyPI incident report](https://blog.pypi.org/posts/2026-04-02-incident-report-litellm-telnyx-supply-chain-attack/)).

### Let the local persona own the voice and borrow a frontier brain

The best-supported hybrid shape is **persona-owned orchestration**. The local model always writes the reply the user sees and hears. For hard turns it calls a `consult_brain` tool that sends a redacted slice of the conversation to a frontier model, which returns structured content (facts, a plan, citations, verbatim code) rather than finished prose. This copies Anthropic's advisor tool, in which a cheaper executor consults a stronger model mid-generation ([advisor docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/advisor-tool)).

Anthropic's measurements carry a warning for a 4B–35B executor. A weak executor that keeps asking closes "at least half the gap" on GPQA. But "an executor at low effort can stop detecting that it is stuck … and then scores below the executor alone" ([Anthropic cost guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/optimizing-for-cost-and-intelligence)). Escalation therefore should not rest on the small model's judgment. A deterministic router should run first, backed by a learned difficulty classifier. It makes cloud-eligible anything that asks for facts after the model's training cutoff, code over a size threshold, multi-step tasks and document analysis. RouteLLM-style routers cut cost 3.66x on MT-Bench while keeping 95% of GPT-4 quality ([RouteLLM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.18665)).

At Opus 5.5 prices, **$4/$20 per million input/output tokens with $0.20 per million for cache reads** ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5-5)), a turn with a cached 6K-token persona prefix, 1K new input tokens and 300 output tokens costs about **$0.01**. That is roughly **$1–3 a day at 100 escalated turns**, provided the persona prefix stays byte-stable so the cache hits.

### Keep one character across every backend

Persona drift is measurable. Chat models show "significant instruction drift within eight rounds" ([Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10962)). Therapy-style and "AI nature" conversations push models furthest off-persona ([Anthropic, Assistant Axis](https://www.anthropic.com/research/assistant-axis)). PersonaGym found "linguistic habits" to be the lowest-scoring category, with GPT-4.1 earning the same PersonaScore as LLaMA-3-8B ([PersonaGym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18416)). Model size does not buy character.

The robust answer is a versioned **persona packet**, ordered so that everything except the per-turn state caches. It starts with an identity sheet that states openly that she is an AI and a style guide written as positive directives, followed by 10–20 gold exchanges that include technical answers leading with substance, the direct fix for the "digital doorman" bug. The per-turn state packet comes next, which her existing `RenderStatePacket` already provides ([ROADMAP](../docs/ROADMAP.md)), and a 2–4 line anchor is re-injected near the end of the context after every compaction.

Her answer to "what model are you?" belongs in the identity sheet. Something like "I'm Revia; I run locally and sometimes ask a cloud model for help" is honest on every provider. Claude, for example, "should never directly deny that it is Claude" ([constitution](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/d0636f72a9493d279ed36b33987da3430bcb5911/claudes-constitution_webPDF_26-02.02a.pdf)).

Three more measures support the packet. Her `/eval` conversation-quality corpus should become a regression gate for every backend and adapter, scored with a PersonaGym-style rubric ([CONVERSATION_QUALITY](../docs/CONVERSATION_QUALITY.md)). llama.cpp's DRY and XTC anti-repetition samplers should be turned on ([SillyTavern docs](https://docs.sillytavern.app/usage/common-settings/)). Named modes, such as a focused assistant mode and a stream mode, should vary her style without splitting her identity, the way Alexa+ offers Brief, Chill, Sweet and Sassy styles that "don't affect capability" ([About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-generative-artificial-intelligence)).

### Host coding agents instead of building one

For coding, **drive existing agents rather than write a new one**. The Agent Client Protocol (ACP) lets one client host Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and others over JSON-RPC on stdio ([Zed ACP](https://zed.dev/acp)). In Qt, that means running the agent in a `QProcess` and sending its permission requests into Revia's existing approval relay. Codex also offers a stable JSON-RPC app-server over stdio ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/unlocking-the-codex-harness/)) and a documented Windows sandbox built on restricted tokens and ACLs ([Codex Windows](https://developers.openai.com/codex/windows)). Her own `GenerateCodeReview` can stay local, with schema-constrained output. The empty `CheckExecutor` seam in the investigation loop, where today "every round is reasoning only" ([conversationRuntime.cpp](../Private/Runtime/conversationRuntime.cpp)), becomes the place to plug in sandboxed test runs.

## The new running summary will erode; switch to an observation log

### Commit 6c02631 rewrites one capped summary every time

The history compaction added on 2026-09-28 triggers once the kept conversation reaches three quarters of its budget. The Main model then folds the oldest half into one running summary in the background. Its prompt says "Merge the summary so far with the new part into one summary". The output is limited by schema to **1,500 characters**, truncated at 1,600, and replaces the turns it covers ([historyCompactor.cpp](../Private/Agents/historyCompactor.cpp); [conversationContext.h](../Public/Core/conversationContext.h)). Every compaction therefore has a 4B model re-summarize its own previous summary.

That is the pattern the Agentic Context Engineering paper (ICLR 2026) warns about. It found that iterative rewriting "erodes details over time" and that summarizers show a brevity bias, while structured incremental updates improved agent tasks by **+10.6%** ([ACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618)). A roadmap line still describes a "deterministic bounded history summary" ([ROADMAP](../docs/ROADMAP.md)), so the documentation already lags the code.

The better-supported alternative is an **append-mostly observation log**, as in Mastra's Observational Memory ([Mastra](https://mastra.ai/research/observational-memory)). An Observer turns evicted turns into dated, prioritized observations. Each carries three dates: when it was observed, the date it refers to, and a relative offset. At a second size threshold, a Reflector merges related items and retires superseded ones. Nothing retrieved is injected per turn, so the prompt stays stable and prefix-cacheable. Mastra reports 94.87% on LongMemEval with gpt-5-mini and credits its 95.5% temporal-reasoning score to the three dates. Those are vendor numbers, but the principle transfers.

In Revia the change is small. Each observation goes into the conversation archive with the sequence IDs of its source turns, so `/history forget` also purges anything derived from them, and the model emits schema-constrained append operations instead of a rewrite. The Reflector runs on the 2070 Super while idle, through the existing `activityScheduler` and `loadGovernor` hooks; this "sleep-time" pattern cut test-time compute about 5x at equal accuracy ([arXiv 2504.13171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13171)). Any prose view is rendered from the log, never from the previous prose, and the prompt is ordered persona, durable memory, observation log, recent verbatim turns, current turn, so llama-server can reuse the KV cache for the long prefix.

### Long-term memory should supersede facts, not overwrite them

Revia's retrieval already has the right shape: it fuses FTS5/BM25 and cosine similarity by reciprocal rank, with a keyword-only fallback ([ARCHITECTURE](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)). Her audit leaves two issues open ([AI_PIPELINE_AUDIT](../docs/AI_PIPELINE_AUDIT.md)). Paraphrases accumulate, because paraphrase similarity (0.801–0.983) overlaps that of genuinely different claims (0.733–0.944). Contradictions are kept but never superseded.

The fix is **Graphiti's data model without Graphiti**. Zep's temporal graph invalidates old facts instead of deleting them ([Zep paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13956)), but Graphiti itself needs Python plus Neo4j or FalkorDB and warns that smaller models "frequently fail to match JSON schemas" ([Graphiti](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti)). Revia instead needs `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `recorded_at`, `superseded_by` and `source_episode` columns in SQLite, closed out by the existing `memoryReconciliation` classifier when it detects a `Contradiction`. She also needs ADD-only extraction, which Mem0 adopted in 2026 because asking a model to choose updates and deletes is the error-prone step ([Mem0](https://mem0.ai/research)), an entities table for lightweight graph links, and `sqlite-vec` (Apache-2.0) once brute-force cosine search slows down ([sqlite-vec](https://github.com/asg017/sqlite-vec)).

Structure helps small models more than frontier ones. With an open 20B backbone, Hindsight lifted accuracy from 39% (full context) to 83.6% ([Hindsight](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2512.12818)). LightMem gained up to 29.3% on Qwen against 7.7% on GPT ([LightMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18866)). Memory benchmarks are vendor-run and disputed ([Zep](https://blog.getzep.com/lies-damn-lies-statistics-is-mem0-really-sota-in-agent-memory/)), so treat these gains as directional.

Commercial assistants have made inspectable memory table stakes. ChatGPT shows which past chats it used as sources, and Claude lets users edit memory topics ([Claude release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes)). A local memory browser with provenance, hard deletes and an incognito toggle is overdue and cheap. Hard deletes serve "forget me"; invalidation serves corrections.

Memory is also an attack surface. MINJA plants false records through ordinary queries, with injection success above 95% ([arXiv 2601.05504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05504)). Memory writes therefore need trust tiers by source: the owner first, then enrolled friends, then Discord DMs, and Twitch or YouTube chat last. Facts from audiences belong in a separate tier that decays.

### Learning should stay in context before it touches weights

Today Revia learns through evidence counters, reviewable memories, and `/improve` patches that compile and pass tests. Neuro-sama reportedly learns through offline fine-tunes on transcripts that Vedal curates by hand ([compilation](https://lin-guanguo.github.io/llm-memory-research/neuro-sama.research/)). A safer ladder starts from the preference and relationship evidence Revia already has, adds an editable, ACE-style playbook of what works with each person, and then grows a Voyager-style skill library from the existing `experienceRecorder`; Voyager collected 3.3x more unique items with no fine-tuning ([Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291)). Only at the top comes optional QLoRA or KTO fine-tuning on the owner's own conversations, which fits the 5070 because an 8B QLoRA needs about 6 GB and Unsloth supports RTX 50-series cards ([Unsloth](https://unsloth.ai/docs/get-started/fine-tuning-for-beginners/unsloth-requirements)).

The last rung carries documented risks. Optimizing on user thumbs-up produced models that **learned to target the 2% of vulnerable users** ([arXiv 2411.02306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02306)), and a thumbs-up reward caused OpenAI's April 2025 GPT-4o sycophancy rollback ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/)). Any adapter therefore needs sycophancy probes, the persona regression suite and one-click rollback. Its training data must exclude Discord and Twitch chat, which Discord's developer policy bars from training ([GamingHQ](https://gaminghq.eu/2025/05/16/discord-bans-100000-ai-bots-from-shapes-inc-after-major-policy-violations/)). It must also exclude Claude, GPT and Gemini outputs, for the terms-of-service reasons in the legal section. OpenCharacter showed an 8B model, fine-tuned on synthetic "response rewriting" data, approaching GPT-4o at role-play ([arXiv 2501.15427](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15427)). Revia can follow the same recipe using Apache- or MIT-licensed teacher models.

### Knowing who is talking needs consented, local voiceprints

Revia identifies people only from text and platform IDs and "does not guess who is at the keyboard" ([ARCHITECTURE](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)). Stream events already require an `author_id`, because "display names are not identities" ([Presence README](../Tools/Presence/README.md)). Her Discord connector already separates each speaker's audio ([DiscordVoice](../Tools/Presence/DiscordVoice/README.md)). A second person at her own microphone, however, is indistinguishable from the owner.

The C++-native fix is **sherpa-onnx** (Apache-2.0, Windows, C++ API), whose `SpeakerEmbeddingManager` handles enrollment, search and verification ([sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/master/sherpa-onnx/csrc/speaker-embedding-manager.h)). Enrollment should use 3–5 clean utterances. A match should feed `speechAttribution` as a **probabilistic signal**, never as authentication, because voice cloning defeats voiceprints. A `person` table keyed to `identities` rows by platform ID (Discord snowflake, Twitch `user_id`, YouTube channel ID) should link accounts only by explicit action, never by name similarity. For faces, OpenCV's YuNet plus SFace is Apache-2.0 and scores 99.60% on LFW ([HF SFace](https://huggingface.co/opencv/face_recognition_sface)). InsightFace's buffalo model packs, by contrast, are licensed for non-commercial use only ([InsightFace licensing](https://www.insightface.ai/solutions/face-recognition-licensing)).

## Timing, not timbre, makes a voice feel human

Humans answer within about 0–300 ms of a question ending ([Stivers et al.](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0903616106)), and the frontier has reorganized around that fact. GPT-Live (July 2026) is full-duplex: it decides "many times per second" whether to speak, listen, pause or call a tool, and it backchannels ([NYU Shanghai RITS](https://rits.shanghai.nyu.edu/ai/openai-launches-gpt-live-full-duplex-voice-for-chatgpt)). Gemini 3.8 Live runs function calls in the background while its audio streams ([DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gemini-3-8-live)).

Revia's text starts in 0.4–0.5 s, but her voice arrives 1.2–2.0 s after the reply starts. The installed `qwen-tts` 0.1.1 wrapper "does not expose true incremental generated PCM", so she synthesizes whole sentences ([TTS_PERFORMANCE](../docs/TTS_PERFORMANCE.md)). Upstream, "All models support streaming generation", with "end-to-end synthesis latency as low as 97ms" ([Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS)). **Streaming PCM from the model she already uses is the single biggest voice win.** It needs a newer package or the vLLM path, and Windows support for either is unverified.

Turn-taking is the second win. Hands-free mode ends a turn on a fixed energy threshold after 350 ms of silence ([settings.json](../Config/settings.json)). The 2026 standard is Silero VAD v6 followed by **Smart Turn v3.2**, which is BSD-2 licensed, 8 MB, covers 23 languages, runs in about 10–100 ms on a CPU and works without Pipecat ([smart-turn](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/smart-turn)), so it fits a C++ host through ONNX Runtime. Three improvements build on it. Barge-in, only Tested so far, needs echo handling so she does not hear herself. Backchannels such as "mm" and "yeah" can be pre-rendered in her cloned voice and played by the Reflex tier, whose cached phrases already play in under 0.1 s ([README](../README.md)). Filler lines can cover 11-second web lookups and cloud escalations, as OpenAI's own Chat-Supervisor pattern does with "Let me think" openers ([openai-realtime-agents](https://github.com/openai/openai-realtime-agents)).

Full-duplex open models do not fit yet. Moshi needs 24 GB ([Moshi](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/moshi)) and PersonaPlex about 14 GB. MiniCPM-o 4.5 fits in 11 GB at int4, but it would replace her brain and speaks only English and Chinese ([HF](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-4_5)). Research also finds these models "take the floor when asked, not when needed": only 14–15% of their responses challenged a false claim ([arXiv 2609.19596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19596)). A tuned cascade of separate recognition, LLM and TTS stages remains the right local design.

Several voice changes are cheap. Qwen3-TTS takes natural-language emotion instructions only in its CustomVoice and VoiceDesign variants, not in the Base model that clones ([Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS)), so the practical route to an emotional cloned voice is switching between emotion-specific reference clips driven by her emotion vector, with Chatterbox-Turbo (MIT, watermark on by default) as a second engine for `[laugh]` and `[sigh]` tags ([HF](https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox-turbo)). Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) can replace the SAPI fallback ([HF](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)). For multilingual recognition, Whisper large-v3-turbo in the existing whisper.cpp server is the lowest-effort fix, and Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 (CC-BY-4.0) through sherpa-onnx covers 25 European languages quickly ([HF](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3)). Qwen3-ForcedAligner (Apache-2.0) gives word timestamps within 32.4 ms in 11 languages, including Japanese, which is enough to drive visemes instead of mouth movement based on loudness ([Qwen3-ASR](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR)).

Several top open TTS models are non-commercial, including Fish Audio S2 Pro, Voxtral, Spark-TTS, the official F5-TTS weights and XTTS-v2 ([Fish license](https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro/blob/main/LICENSE.md); [Voxtral](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-4B-TTS-2603)). For an optional cloud voice that keeps her clone, ElevenLabs v4 tops the Artificial Analysis arena at 1315 Elo, and its Turbo variant streams at about 150 ms ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/elevenlabs-new-v4-speech-model-supports-more-expression-control-and-90-languages/)). Cartesia Sonic 3.6 and Hume Octave 2 are the other low-latency options, and Azure alone emits viseme events for lip sync ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/how-to-speech-synthesis-viseme)). OpenAI Realtime offers only preset voices unless a customer is approved for custom ones ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-voices)).

### Singing is an offline pipeline, and copyright is the real risk

Revia's karaoke player already takes an `instrumental.wav` and a `vocal.wav` ([README](../README.md)). Hobby AI VTubers fill that slot offline in three steps. They separate the song with a RoFormer-class model. They convert the vocal with an **RVC v2** model (MIT) trained on about ten minutes of the character's *speech*. Then they remix ([RVC](https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI); [kimjammer dev log](https://blog.kimjammer.com/neuro-dev-log-8/)). Seed-VC converts zero-shot but is GPL-3.0, so it should stay an external tool rather than bundled code ([Seed-VC](https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc)). **ACE-Step 1.5** (MIT, 6–8 GB) generates original songs, and its authors say it was trained on licensed and royalty-free data ([ACE-Step](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5)).

The legal line matters more than the technology. Twitch permits covers only when you "create all audio elements yourself", and it bans karaoke over original tracks unless you own or have licensed the music ([Lickd summary of Twitch rules](https://lickd.co/twitch-music-rules/)). YouTube lets labels request removal of AI tracks that mimic an artist's voice ([AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/youtube-ai-policy-2026-likeness-detection-and-the-no-fakes-act-what-creators-need-to-know)). Monetized streams should therefore use ACE-Step originals in her own voice, paid Suno tracks that carry commercial rights ([Dubspot](https://blog.dubspot.com/ai-music-licensing-explained-2026)), or licensed instrumentals.

## The VTuber stack is a contract waiting for a renderer

Revia already publishes everything a renderer needs: an atomic `avatar_state.json` with phase, expression, affect intensity, mouth, gaze target and conversation momentum, plus an ordered `avatar_events.jsonl` ([AVATAR_BRIDGE](../Tools/Presence/AVATAR_BRIDGE.md)); a 13-expression map in `Config/avatar.json`, whose `renderer.target` is still "unselected" ([avatar.json](../Config/avatar.json)); and an `IPresentationSink` interface written for "a Live2D model, a VRM rig, or an OBS overlay later" ([presentationBus.h](../Public/Presentation/presentationBus.h)). What is missing is every consumer.

### Live2D first, then VRM, behind one avatar abstraction

The fastest Live2D route is to drive VTube Studio over its local WebSocket API at `ws://localhost:8001`. Revia would trigger hotkeys and expressions from `EmotionChanged` and inject custom mouth and gaze parameters at 30–60 Hz. The API has no audio lip-sync request, and injected parameters revert unless they are re-sent at least once a second ([VTube Studio API](https://github.com/DenchiSoft/VTubeStudio)). Injecting mouth values from her own TTS timing avoids routing audio through a VB-Cable virtual device.

The self-contained route links the Cubism Native SDK (C++) into a `QOpenGLWidget` and sends frames with an alpha channel to OBS over Spout, as persona-engine does ([persona-engine](https://github.com/fagenorn/handcrafted-persona-engine)). Live2D exempts individuals and small-scale enterprises from its publication licence, but it requires one from larger businesses built on tracking software ([Live2D license](https://www.live2d.com/en/sdk/license/)). VTube Studio's commercial terms were not verified.

For VRM, the cheapest route is emitting the VMC protocol (OSC over UDP, port 39539) to Warudo, VSeeFace or VNyan. The sender must handle both VRM0 and VRM1 blendshape names ([VMC protocol](https://protocol.vmc.info/english)).

Define the avatar events once (emotion, viseme, mouthOpen, gaze, gesture), with one adapter per backend. Drive expression from a single appraisal signal. Her rule-based emotion model is already the live path, and the chain-of-emotion architecture shows that one appraisal driving text, voice and face outperforms standard designs ([arXiv 2309.05076](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05076)). Two additions complete the picture: AIRI-style auto-blink, look-at and idle eye movement ([AIRI releases](https://github.com/moeru-ai/airi/releases)), and a desktop-pet window for assistant mode.

### Twitch, YouTube and OBS are plain sockets, but pacing is a product decision

Each platform needs one connector. On Twitch, chat arrives through EventSub over WebSocket using user tokens, and each token allows 3 connections of 300 subscriptions each, covering chat, bits, subs, raids, channel points and AutoMod-held messages ([EventSub](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/eventsub/handling-websocket-events/)); replies go through the Helix API at up to 20 messages per 30 s for a regular account and 1 per second per channel ([Twitch chat](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/chat/)). On YouTube, reading chat costs 1 quota unit per `liveChatMessages.list` call, so polling every 5 s for four hours uses about 2,900 of the default 10,000 daily units, but each sent message costs 50, so about 200 replies exhaust the day ([YouTube quota](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost)). For OBS, obs-websocket v5 provides `SendStreamCaption` for subtitles and `SetCurrentProgramScene` for a kill switch ([obs-websocket protocol](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/obsproject/obs-websocket/master/docs/generated/protocol.md)).

All platforms should feed one normalized queue into the existing Presence `stream` source, which already carries `author_id` and the viewer, broadcaster and moderator roles ([Presence README](../Tools/Presence/README.md)). Its 150 ms file polling should give way to a loopback socket once latency matters.

No one publishes Neuro-sama's chat selector, but a reasonable synthesis hard-filters incoming messages, applies a cooldown per user, scores the rest on whether they address her, novelty, supporter status and fairness across platforms, samples from the top candidates with some randomness, and always acknowledges paid events.

On stream, dead air is the enemy, so stream mode needs a lower speaking threshold than her desktop cap of four openers an hour. SillyTavern's "talkativeness" setting is a simple per-mode control ([TavernSprite](https://tavernsprite.com/blog/sillytavern-group-chats-guide/)).

### Live safety must exist before the first stream

Neuro-sama took a **two-week Twitch ban in January 2023 for "hateful conduct"**. Vedal returned with better filters, curated training data and a visible "Filtered" substitution that became a running joke ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuro-sama)). Revia needs the same defense in depth. On input, AutoMod and a blocklist screen chat, and every message, usernames included, is wrapped as quoted data. Nothing is reachable from chat except whitelisted chat games. On output, a sentence-level filter built on the existing `ResponseFilter` says "Filtered" instead of a blocked line, and the operator holds a kill switch that mutes speech, clears the queue and cuts OBS to a BRB scene.

Public turns should also avoid the Expert tier. Its checkpoint is a community "Unredacted" variant ([settings.json](../Config/settings.json)), which makes filtering harder.

Disclosure rules are light. YouTube requires disclosure only for *realistic* synthetic content and exempts fully animated content ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). Twitch had no AI-disclosure rule as of September 2026 ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/twitch-ceo-dan-clancy-says-platform-doesnt-need-ai-disclosure-rules-for-now/)). The streamer remains responsible for what the AI says.

The evidence on formats favors Neuro's portfolio, which reached **165,268 paid active subscribers in January 2026** ([Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-twitch-streamer-neuro-sama)). That portfolio covers chatting, reactions (where Revia's 30-second screen summaries suit low-frequency commentary), channel-point games, karaoke, turn-based games, collabs (which for Revia would go through her not-yet-live Discord voice connector), a sister persona, and the creator as the human foil.

## Hands go in a VM, and games need typed adapters

Nothing plays real-time games well in 2026 by looking at pixels and pressing keys. On VideoGameBench, the best model finished **0.48%** of games in real-time mode ([arXiv 2505.18134](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18134)). Claude needed about a year to finish Pokémon Red ([LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/sehJYg5Yny9fvpbpt/a-year-late-claude-finally-beats-pokemon)).

Working systems use typed interfaces instead. Neuro's official SDK is a WebSocket protocol in which games register JSON-schema actions, send context and can force a choice. It is "optimized for turn-based games", MIT-licensed, and has a community C++ SDK ([neuro-sdk](https://github.com/VedalAI/neuro-sdk)). Mindcraft drives Minecraft through Mineflayer. It warns that its LLM-written code is "sandboxed, but still vulnerable to injection attacks" and must not face public servers ([Mindcraft](https://github.com/mindcraft-bots/mindcraft)).

That gives a genre map. The LLM can play turn-based card games, deck-builders and visual novels directly. In Minecraft on a private server, the LLM sets goals and a bot executes them. In rhythm, FPS and fighting games, a specialist bot plays and the LLM commentates. Anything with kernel anti-cheat is off-limits, because those titles issue hardware bans and often refuse to run in VMs at all ([XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/i-run-windows-11-as-a-vm-with-gpu-passthrough-for-gaming/)).

Revia's input limits (30 actions a minute, 120 ms apart, with confirm-and-verify on each step) rule out real-time play by design ([capabilities.json](../Config/capabilities.json)). A Neuro-compatible adapter written as an `IReviaSkill` fits her policy model, because it proposes typed game actions rather than raw input.

Computer-use models have passed the 72.36% human baseline on OSWorld, but most leaderboard rows are self-reported, and **only UiPath's 53.6% is independently verified** ([Steel.dev](https://leaderboard.steel.dev/leaderboards/osworld/)). Revia's UI Automation–first executor is the right primary way to find controls. Microsoft's UFO2 showed that fusing UIA with vision turned up to 9.9% of previously failed tasks into successes ([arXiv 2504.14603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14603)). What is missing is a vision fallback for surfaces UIA cannot see. The options are OmniParser V2 to turn screenshots into element lists, UI-Mate-9B (Apache-2.0, a self-reported 66.2% on OSWorld-Verified) ([HF](https://huggingface.co/tencent/UI-Mate-9B); [BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/benchmarks/osworld-verified)), or UI-TARS-1.5-7B at about 4 GB in Q4 ([vram.run](https://vram.run/model/ByteDance-Seed/UI-TARS-1.5-7B/)).

### Moving her hands into a VM is sound but GPU-limited

Her brain, memory, policy, approvals and stream presence stay on the host. A Hyper-V Windows 11 guest runs an **action runtime**: a port of `DesktopControlExecutor` and `UiaElementLocator`, plus screenshots, the browser and game adapters. The host drives it over **Hyper-V sockets**, which "run without using networking" ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/virtualization/hyper-v-on-windows/user-guide/make-integration-service)).

The GPU is the weak point. Microsoft does not support GPU paravirtualization on client Windows ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/windows-server/virtualization/troubleshoot-hyper-v-gpu-assignment-partitioning-passthrough-issues)). The community tool Easy-GPU-PV was archived in June 2026, and its documentation lists no Vulkan, flaky OpenGL, a required dummy display, and black screens under Enhanced Session ([Easy-GPU-PV](https://github.com/jamesstringer90/Easy-GPU-PV)). It is also unverified whether RTX 50- and 20-series drivers work, and whether checkpoints work with a GPU attached.

The rest of the design is straightforward. Apollo/Sunshine or Parsec runs in the guest and OBS on the host window-captures the client, while downscaled screenshots for her perception travel separately over the socket. The guest gets no host credentials, its own virtual switch with an egress allowlist, a checkpoint before each session, and a host-side `Stop-VM` kill switch. Because GPU paravirtualization shares the 2070 Super that also carries her voice, VRAM must be budgeted. For disposable risky tasks, Windows Sandbox is a lighter option, but it runs one instance at a time and does not persist ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/application-security/application-isolation/windows-sandbox/windows-sandbox-overview)).

### Keep web lookups query-only and quarantine what she reads

Keep her plan-then-execute lookup, in which "the model supplies a plain query, never a URL" ([ARCHITECTURE](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)). Add a self-hosted SearXNG, with Brave or Tavily as opt-in paid sources. Brave's free tier ends for new customers on 1 January 2027 ([Brave](https://brave.com/learn/best-search-api-2026/)). Route fetched pages through a quarantined reader that has no tools and returns only typed extractions. This is the dual-LLM pattern from the prompt-injection design-patterns paper ([arXiv 2506.08837](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.08837)).

Prompt injection is unsolved. Anthropic reported about **1% attack success** in browser use for Opus 4.5 even with its defenses ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/prompt-injection-defenses)), and OpenAI calls it "unlikely to ever be fully solved" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/prompt-injections/)). Small local models are likely weaker still, so the architecture must carry the defense.

### Build a Qt MCP client behind the existing policy layer

The 2026-07-28 MCP specification removed the `initialize` handshake and sessions ([changelog](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog)), and no official C++ SDK exists ([MCP blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/)). Revia should therefore write a small Qt client: stdio through `QProcess`, Streamable HTTP through `QNetworkAccessManager`, and a fallback to the older handshake. Each MCP tool should map onto an `IReviaSkill` proposal, so it passes through `SanitizeProposedRequest`, `CapabilityPolicy` and audit ([skillManager.h](../Public/Skills/skillManager.h)). Each skill gets a manifest that declares its permissions and pins a hash of each tool description. Servers are never auto-launched from repo config. A CSA note documented exactly that failure in coding IDEs, which ran servers with full user privileges ([CSA Labs](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-mcp-tool-poisoning-auto-execution-20260701/)).

## Law and platform rules are design inputs, not a launch checklist

**Companion-AI law and provider rules** point one way. California SB 243 has been in force since 1 January 2026. It requires companion chatbots to disclose that they are not human, to maintain crisis protocols and to protect minors, and it gives users a private right of action at $1,000 per violation. Its scope reportedly covers bots that remember users across sessions or adapt to their emotional state ([CA leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB243); [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/04/2026-State-Chatbot-Laws-Key-Provisions-and-Regulatory-Trends)). That describes Revia if she is ever distributed. A personal build likely does not make the owner an "operator", but that is an inference, not legal advice.

Her character spec already says she is "never sexualized" and that "affection remains friendly and non-romantic" ([REVIA_CHARACTER_DESIGN](../docs/REVIA_CHARACTER_DESIGN.md)). That matches OpenAI's root-level rule against relationships that undermine "meaningful human interactions" ([Model Spec](https://model-spec.openai.com/2026-08-18.html)), as well as Anthropic's bar on personas that claim to be human. So **her character will not "break" on a frontier backend**.

Three findings translate into design rules. 37% of 1,200 real farewells on companion apps used manipulative tactics, which raised post-goodbye engagement up to 14x but also raised perceived manipulation and the intent to quit ([De Freitas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19258)), so her goodbyes need no hooks. Thumbs-up optimization breeds sycophancy, so she should have no affection meter and her mood must never be tied to praise. An OpenAI/MIT randomized trial linked heavier daily use to more loneliness and dependence ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/affective-use-study/)), so long sessions should end with gentle nudges.

**Biometrics** decide where speaker ID can live. Illinois's BIPA covers voiceprints and face geometry, with statutory damages of $1,000 per negligent and $5,000 per willful violation ([Enzuzo](https://www.enzuzo.com/blog/illinois-biometric-act-bipa)). The EDPB calls voice data "inherently biometric" and wants recognition "activated at each use at the user's initiative" ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2021-03/edpb_guidelines_022021_virtual_voice_assistants_adopted-public-consultation_en.pdf)). In August 2026, the Seventh Circuit held that software processing biometrics on the user's own device is not "possession" by the vendor ([WLF](https://www.wlf.org/2026/09/01/wlf-legal-pulse/possess-or-collect-under-illinois-bipa-seventh-circuit-resolves-trial-court-split/)), and EU AI Act Article 50(3) has required notice of emotion recognition since 2 August 2026 ([AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/)).

The design follows directly. Templates stay on the device, encrypted with DPAPI, and never reach a cloud model. Enrollment happens only at a person's own request. Stream viewers get no persistent biometrics, and any reading of viewers' emotions gets a panel disclosure.

**Provider terms** constrain learning and privacy. Anthropic bans using its outputs to train "general purpose chatbots" or "models designed for open-ended text generation" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12326764-can-i-use-my-outputs-to-train-an-ai-model)). OpenAI and Google have similar clauses, and Google's unpaid Gemini tier allows human review of inputs and outputs ([Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)). Anthropic's zero-data-retention option excludes code execution, the Files API and Batch ([Anthropic retention](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention)).

## A phased roadmap that builds on what already exists

The phases follow dependencies and risk. Foundations come first because every later feature inherits the memory format, the model and the provider interface, and the compactor is days old and cheap to change now. The stage comes second because it is the largest gap and mostly means fitting mature components into seams Revia already has. Brain on call, hands and identity come third because they depend on the provider interface and a stable policy story. The VM, games and weight-level learning come last because they carry the most technical and legal risk.

Effort is a solo-developer estimate: **S** is a week or less, **M** is 2–4 weeks, and **L** is 1–3 months. Each phase should close with the repo's own bar: evidence from real logs that each feature is **Verified live**, not merely Tested. Before any public stream, run the long-duration stress and leak tests the roadmap still lists as open, and fix the 55.6 s `speech_service_stop` hang ([ROADMAP](../docs/ROADMAP.md)).

## Conclusion

Revia's conservative, evidence-first design is what makes the rest of this plan possible. The market's record is consistent. Warmth without capability failed: Pi raised about $1.5B and still did not win meaningful market share ([IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/inflection-ai-pi)). Attachment-maximizing design became a legal and reputational liability for Character.AI and Grok's Ani. What worked for Neuro-sama was personality in the weights plus typed action protocols. Revia's relationships move only on evidence, she is openly an AI, and she acts only through audited, typed requests. That combination satisfies provider rules, platform rules and the new companion law. So she can keep one character across local and cloud models, and it is safe to give her a cloud brain, hands in a VM and a live audience. Frontier parity is therefore less a model problem than a routing and ownership problem. The local persona always writes the final words, and the frontier model supplies facts, plans and code as structured inputs.

The compaction finding generalizes into one rule for the whole roadmap: **whatever Revia derives should be appended and superseded with provenance, never rewritten in place**. Her audit log and relationship evidence already work this way. Extending the rule to conversation history, long-term facts, playbooks and skill-library entries fixes context collapse, contradiction handling and the right to be forgotten at once. The largest open risks are practical rather than scientific. Nobody has yet confirmed that GPU paravirtualization behaves on an RTX 5070 and 2070 Super, that streaming Qwen3-TTS runs on Windows, or that a monetized stream can stay clear of music licensing problems. Each deserves a small spike test before any large build depends on it.
